TV Setup

Not paired

This page runs on the TV itself, one install only. Pair your phone once, then the steps below run on the TV.

On the TV, choose Allow with the remote (press Right, then OK)…

Checking local debugging…

  1. On the TV, open Settings, Developer options, turn on network debugging.
  2. Come back here and tap Check again. The first time, the TV asks Allow USB debugging, tick Always allow.

1. Backup

Save the current apps, docks and hidden lists before you change anything.



  
2. Disable apps / junk apps

  
Pick the junk apps to turn off. They are disabled, not deleted, so Restore can bring them back one by one.

  

  

  
All apps and services: search, pick any enabled app or service, then turn it off. Protected core packages are not listed.

  
  

  


  
3. Users

  
No users are built in. Add your own here, then set their docks and hidden lists below. Rename or delete a user here too.

  

  
  
  


  
Weather location for the home screen. Type a city, or a latitude,longitude. No city is built in.

  

  
  
  


  
4. Docks

  
Pick up to 5 apps per user. Tap a user, tap apps, then Save dock.

  

  

  


  
5. Hidden apps

  
Pick the apps this user should not see on the home screen. They still live in that user's Hidden folder.

  

  


  
6. Set as home

  
Turn off the stock home app, so this launcher becomes the home. Restore can bring it back.

  


  
Restore disabled apps

  
Pick one disabled app to bring back, or restore all at the top.
